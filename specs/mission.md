## Core Objective
Build a personalized, privacy-conscious fitness and wellness assistant that intelligently adapts to Devpreyo's specific health goals, dietary restrictions, and location.

*   **Privacy-First Architecture:** While utilizing cloud-based reasoning, all sensitive health metrics, demographic profiles, and progress histories remain strictly consolidated within a local `data/memory.json` file — no external database or cloud storage is involved.
*   **Actionable Intelligence:** The agent must translate raw data into dynamic, actionable routines—providing customized workouts and macro-aligned meal plans rather than generic advice.
*   **Contextual Awareness:** The system will seamlessly blend ongoing conversational guidance with real-world spatial awareness to recommend nearby fitness facilities.
